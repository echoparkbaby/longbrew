# Longbrew app icon

The app icon is an ivory coffee mug with a green battery emblem on a dark teal tile.
Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. `AppIcon-source.png` preserves
the generated master; `AppIcon-1024.png` is the macOS 1024-pixel export.

Run `swift scripts/make-icon.swift` from the project root to regenerate every app
icon size. The menu-bar state glyphs are separate and are not replaced by this art.

## Generation prompt

Create a polished final macOS application icon for Longbrew, a coffee themed keep-awake utility. Single square 1024x1024 image, not a mockup or contact sheet. Premium restrained 3D product illustration with beautifully clean geometry. A large ivory porcelain COFFEE MUG centered, substantial rounded body with an elegant visible handle on the right, subtly visible dark caffeinated surface at the rim. On the FRONT of the mug integrate a prominent horizontal bright mint-green BATTERY symbol with a short terminal on its right, a dark teal lightning bolt centered inside. Battery is unmistakably a battery (rounded rectangular capsule with terminal), not just a bolt. Two graceful short warm-ivory steam ribbons above the cup. Dark petrol-teal rounded-square macOS icon tile, subtle refined gradient, soft warm studio lighting, gently beveled porcelain with realistic soft highlights and a grounded shadow, bold clean silhouette. Mug and battery fill the icon strongly and remain readable at small sizes. Modern elegant icon craftsmanship, warm cream and emerald accents. No text, no letters, no branding, no clutter, no extra objects, no pedestal, no coffee beans. Tile occupies about 88 percent of canvas with even transparent margin and rounded corners; transparent outside the tile. Crisp professional shipping app icon.
